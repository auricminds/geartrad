# GearTrad Marketing Playbook

> Target markets: Egypt, Saudi Arabia, UAE, Kuwait, Bahrain, Qatar, Oman  
> Primary audience: Arabic-speaking gamers aged 16–30  
> Current channels: Instagram (@geartrad) | Discord (in progress)

---

## Phase 1 — Launch (Month 1–2)

### Goal: Get the first 100 real users and 20 listings

---

### Instagram Strategy

**Profile setup:**
- Bio: exactly as we agreed (150 chars, Egypt's #1 gaming marketplace)
- Link in bio: geartrad.com
- Profile pic: GearTrad logo
- Highlights: How It Works, Safety, Top Listings, Reviews

**Content pillars (rotate these):**

| Type | Format | Frequency |
|---|---|---|
| Listing showcase | Reel or carousel — "This Valorant account just dropped 🔥" | 4x/week |
| Gamer memes/culture | Relatable Arabic gaming humor | 3x/week |
| Trust content | "How escrow works" explainers, safety tips | 2x/week |
| User-generated | Screenshot a completed trade, tag the buyer/seller | 1x/week |
| Giveaway | Follow + tag 2 friends to win a boosted listing | 1x/month |

**Reels ideas that work in this niche:**
- "POV: you sold your Valorant account for 500 EGP in 10 minutes"
- "Rating Fortnite accounts for sale on GearTrad 👀"
- "How to sell your CS2 account safely in Egypt"
- Before/after: "I had this account collecting dust… now it's 300 EGP in my pocket"

**Hashtags (mix per post):**
```
#GearTrad #جيرتراد #مصر_جيمز #خليج_جيمز #فالورانت_مصر
#فورتنايت_عربي #CS2 #بيع_حسابات_العاب #سوق_الالعاب
#gaming #valorant #fortnite #FIFA #Egypt #SaudiGaming
```

---

### Discord Strategy (when ready)

**Server structure:**
- `#announcements` — new listings, features, updates
- `#listings-showcase` — sellers post their listings
- `#looking-to-buy` — buyers post what they need
- `#valorant` / `#fortnite` / `#cs2` / `#fifa` — game-specific channels
- `#support` — help and questions
- `#off-topic` — community chat in Arabic + English

**Growth:**
- Link Discord in every Instagram bio and story
- Give early Discord members a "Founding Member" badge on GearTrad
- Run Discord-exclusive deals (10% off listing boost fee for server members)

## Phase 2 — Growth (Month 3–6)

### Influencer Outreach

Target micro-influencers (5k–50k followers) in the gaming niche:

| Country | What to look for |
|---|---|
| Egypt | Arabic gaming Reels creators, Valorant/FIFA streamers |
| Saudi Arabia | Gaming YouTubers, Twitch streamers |
| UAE | Multilingual gaming content creators |

**Offer:** Free boosted listing + commission (5–10%) on every sale they refer via a promo code.

**DM template:**
> "مرحبا [اسم]، أنا [اسمك] من GearTrad — سوق بيع وشراء حسابات الألعاب في مصر والخليج. لو مهتم تتعاون معنا وتكسب عمولة على كل صفقة من خلالك، ابعتلنا DM أو تواصل معنا."

---

### TikTok (when ready)

- Repurpose Instagram Reels to TikTok
- TikTok Arabic gaming is massive in Egypt and Gulf — less saturated than Instagram
- Post consistently: 1–2 videos/day in the first month to hack the algorithm
- Content that goes viral in this niche: account price reveals, "Is this worth it?" reactions, trade timers

---

### YouTube (when ready)

- Longer format: "How to sell your Valorant account in Egypt (step by step)"
- These rank on Google — SEO value is huge for Egypt/Gulf searches
- One good video can drive traffic for years

---

## Phase 3 — Scale (Month 6+)

### Paid Ads

**Meta Ads (Instagram/Facebook):**
- Target: Egypt (18–28, interest: Valorant, Fortnite, CS2, FIFA, online gaming)
- Target: Saudi Arabia + UAE (18–30, same interests)
- Budget to start: 100–200 EGP/day
- Best performing ad type: Reel showing a completed trade ("He sold his account in 8 minutes")

**Google Ads:**
- Target keywords: "بيع حساب فالورانت مصر", "buy valorant account Egypt", "sell fortnite account Saudi"
- These have real search volume and low competition

## Quick Wins (Do This Week)

1. Post your first Reel introducing GearTrad — show the website, show how to list
2. DM 20 gaming pages in Egypt asking for a story shoutout exchange
3. Post in Egyptian gaming Facebook groups (there are massive ones: "جيمرز مصر", "PC Gamers Egypt")
4. Join Arabic Discord gaming servers and mention GearTrad in relevant convos
5. Get 5 friends to list something — even if it's not for sale, to fill the site with real listings

---

## Brand Voice

- Arabic-first but English is fine for game names
- Tone: friendly, hype, trustworthy — like a gamer talking to another gamer
- Always emphasize: **safety**, **speed**, **Egypt + Gulf focus**
- Avoid: corporate language, formal Arabic
